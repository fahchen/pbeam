## Measurement record

This section records the gate of task [#631](https://github.com/fahchen/pbeam/issues/631), proposal 5 of the attribution of task [#625](https://github.com/fahchen/pbeam/issues/625). A `move` from a register copies its term, so a move of a boxed term increments a reference count, and the later write of the dead source decrements it. ERTS copies one word for a `move` (`emit_i_move`, `erts/emulator/beam/jit/arm/instr_common.cpp:516-519`, OTP 29.1.1). The change does not land.

## The change

Commit `4fc08860` adds `Instruction::MoveTake`. The loader marks a `move` from an X or Y register when no later instruction on its straight path reads the register before a write that ends it. A call ends an X register at or above its arity, and `return` ends each X register except `x0`, as the BEAM validator keeps only `x0` after a body call and no register after a tail call or `return` (`schedule_out(0, ...)`, `verify_return`, and `kill_state`, `lib/compiler/src/beam_validator.erl:1204-1257` and `1584-1586`). `deallocate` and a tail call end a Y register, and `trim` moves it to a lower index. The analysis stops with "live" at each instruction that can branch, raise, or read registers that are not its operands, so the implicit reads keep their registers: the arguments of a `func_info` failure, the argument lists of `undef` and `badarity`, the stack traces, the `catch` handlers, and the start arity of an undefined initial call. A non-tail call stops the analysis of a Y register, because a `catch` handler of the same frame can read it after an exception. The interpreter takes the term out of a marked source with no reference count change. `mise run check-brief` passed with 16978 of 16978 application lines, and the error-reason tests and the pinned ERTS comparisons passed unchanged.

## The probe of a boxed move

The `map_get/2` shape keeps the map in `x2` and does not move it. Thus the floor deltas do not come from takes.

A third interleaved session timed a scratch probe module that moves a term in each iteration. Its loop is `loop(N, T) -> X = id(N), loop(X - 1, T)`, compiled by the pinned `erlc`, which gives `move {x,1},{y,0}` before `call 1` and `move {y,0},{x,1}` before `call_last 2`. ## The decision

The change does not land. Its target is the Terra key press, and the two sessions show no gain beyond the session noise. The rounds of one binary span 11.667 ms to 16.500 ms. A build of other work probably overlapped round 1 of `4fc08860` in the second session (7.500 ms, 0.45 s of CPU time for 60 keys), so the record uses rounds 2 to 8. Their after mean is 0.191 ms (1.3 percent) higher, and their after median is 0.167 ms (1.1 percent) lower. The Terra harness cannot resolve a change below about 1 ms for each key, which is more than the 5.9 percent (about 0.86 ms) that the attribution of task #625 gave to the clone and the drop of `Move`. The floor shapes run no `move` in their loop, so they do not measure the change. Their falls of 0.2 ns to 3.8 ns come from other differences of the two binaries. The probe of a boxed move gives about 1.6 ns for each take with a warm cache, or about 0.04 ms (0.3 percent) for the 27,472 takes of a key press. If all of the 8.4 ns fall of the `boxed` probe came from the two takes, the result is 4.2 ns for each take, or about 0.12 ms (0.8 percent) for each key press. Both values are much smaller than the 5.9 percent of #625. The take can save more when the reference count of the term is not in the cache, and the record does not measure that case. A static analysis of about 210 lines of loader code and comments, which each new instruction must keep correct, does not pay for a gain that the gate cannot show and that the probe bounds below 1 percent with a warm cache. Commit `c58b8789` reverts the change, and `docs/backlog.md` keeps it with a trigger.

## Known gaps

- The Terra harness cannot resolve a change below about 1 ms for each key, so the record does not bound the Terra effect of the change.
- The probe of a boxed move measures a take with a warm cache only. It does not measure a take of a term whose reference count is not in the cache, which is the case that the samples of #625 can hold.
- The probe time for each iteration includes the start of `pbeam` and the load of the module. This part is the same for the two revisions, and the `boxed` minus `small` difference removes it.
- The count comes from a scratch probe build, not from the measured binary.
